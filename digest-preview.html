#1  ·  Research

The World Won't Wait: Evaluating and Teaching Agents Under a Live Clock

ArXiv CS.AI  ·  Score 9/10

The authors introduce RealtimeBench, an evaluation suite consisting of 180 tasks across six game types where environments continue running during LLM reasoning and tool calls. Paired evaluations reveal that although GPT-6.1-sol beats the faster model Jev when environments are paused, it falls behind when games continue running under a live clock.

Athanor: An Agent-Native System for Autonomous Post-Training Research

ArXiv CS.AI  ·  Score 9/10

This work presents Athanor, an open agent-native system enabling autonomous post-training research (AutoPostTrain) on real GPUs. The framework grants frontier agents autonomy to post-train small base models using public data, providing a practical testbed for recursive self-improvement capabilities.

#3  ·  Research

MAI-Gym: An Open All-in-One Platform for GUI Agent Evaluation and RL Training across Mobile and Computer Environments

ArXiv CS.AI  ·  Score 8/10

Researchers introduce MAI-Gym, an open all-in-one platform unifying GUI agent evaluation, data collection, and online reinforcement learning training across mobile, computer, and web environments. The platform addresses the scarcity of realistic training environments and tasks required to scale online RL with verifiable rewards.

#4  ·  Research

Failure Is the Mother of Success: Recovering Supervision with Grounding Hindsight

ArXiv CS.AI  ·  Score 8/10

When all rollouts for a problem fail, standard GRPO generates zero group-relative advantage and learns nothing from the hardest tasks. To resolve this, Replay-Selected Hindsight Distillation (RSHD) generates corrective notes from failed rollouts to extract and test an additional supervision signal during GRPO training.

#5  ·  Research

Evaluating Model Capabilities After Benchmark Saturation

ArXiv CS.AI  ·  Score 8/10

Rapid AI capability gains are causing existing benchmarks to saturate, preventing scalar scores from differentiating top models or informing AI safety cases. The study explores evaluating models beyond scalar scores by analyzing the full execution transcripts produced when models solve benchmark tasks.
